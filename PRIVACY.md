# Hindsight — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 6, 2026_

Hindsight is an Atlassian Forge app that predicts a post-mortem for a Jira issue before it ships.

**What data the app reads (live, on demand)**
- Jira issue fields (key, summary, components, labels, assignee display name) and past resolved issues in the same project.
- Confluence page titles and last-modified dates, to judge runbook freshness.

**What the app does NOT do**
- It does not store personal data. Nothing is saved to Forge storage or any database.
- It does not send data outside Atlassian. The app runs entirely on Atlassian Forge, with no external network calls (no egress).
- It does not use your data to train any model.

**What the app writes**
- Only when you click "Create mitigation subtasks" (or confirm in the Rovo agent), it creates Jira subtasks under the chosen issue.

**Logs**
- Forge may keep standard runtime logs for troubleshooting, governed by Atlassian's Forge policies.
